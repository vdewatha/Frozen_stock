---
name: Overview priorities
description: Product intent for the approved positions-and-strategies overview
---

Prioritize the current broker book and strategy ownership on the Overview, with research and account metrics secondary. Keep missing ownership and unverified P/L explicit rather than substituting illustrative data.

**Why:** The user approved the positions-and-strategies redesign to make current holdings and strategy activity easy to understand, not to change execution.

**How to apply:** Preserve this information hierarchy in future Overview revisions. Display changes must not reconcile accounts, place orders, or alter trading controls.
