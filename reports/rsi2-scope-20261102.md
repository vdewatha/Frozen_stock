# RSI(2) Pullback: Entry Threshold and Symbol Scope, 2026-11-02

**Questions:** should RSI(2) Pullback enter at RSI < 5 instead of RSI < 10, and should it trade single stocks
instead of only SPY, QQQ, IWM and DIA?

**Decisions:**
* **Keep RSI < 10.** RSI < 5 wins on the full history but not in the recent windows, and it halves the trade
  count. On stocks since 2020 it earns +8.5 bps per trade against +14.8 for RSI < 10. On index ETFs since 2020 the
  two tie at +23 bps.
* **Widen the scope to the 29 single stocks** of the paper universe, keeping the index ETFs. With RSI < 10, stocks
  earn about what index ETFs do since 2015 (+16.6 vs +13.7 bps) on 6.6x the trades. Sector ETFs stay out: they
  earn +4 to +9 bps.

None of the post-2015 confidence intervals exclude zero, so this is an exploration change. The trades stay small,
tagged exploration orders and must still earn promotion through the scorecard. The stock results also favor names
that are in today's universe because they did well (survivorship bias).

## Method

* Data: yfinance daily OHLCV, split and dividend adjusted, from each symbol's first bar through 2026-10-30.
* Engine: `strategy_scorecard.score_cell` / `pool_variant` (the shared backtester): signals on completed bars,
  next-open fills, volatility-scaled costs on both sides.
* Settings: the production RSI(2) row, meaning the 200-day trend filter, exit on a close above the 5-day SMA, a 10%
  catastrophe stop, no target and a 10-session time stop.
* Expectancy is net bps per trade with a 95% cluster bootstrap CI by entry month (1,000 draws).
