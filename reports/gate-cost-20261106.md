# Gate cost audit, 2026-11-06

Branch `claude/gate-cost-audit-31a009`, roadmap item 1 of `self-evolution-roadmap-20261106.md`.
**Question:** every entry gate (SPY-trend market gate, VIX gate, ADX regime gate, stop cooldown, earnings and
announcement blackouts, one position per symbol, sector cap, ...) earned its verdict on the trades it let
through. What did the trades it *stopped* cost?

**Goal used:** total profit after costs. A gate pays for itself when the trades it blocks lose money on
average; it costs money when they win.

## What was built

`app/services/gate_cost.py`, paper only; nothing here places, blocks or exits an order.

1. **Blocked trades.** The autotrader already records every withheld BUY as a `buy_signal_outcomes` audit row
   (symbol, strategy, status, reason). Each reason is mapped to a gate name (`gate_for_reason`): the entry
   protection name (`market_gate`, `stop_cooldown`, ...), `vix_gate`, `regime_gate`, `earnings_blackout`,
   `announcement_blackout`, `one_position_per_symbol`, `sector_cap`, `meta_label`, and the cycle's own
   bookkeeping blocks (`exit_pending`, `no_pyramiding`, `exit_legs_live`). The last 60 sessions
   (`gate_cost_lookback_sessions`) are deduplicated to one `(symbol, strategy, session, gate)` per session,
   because the cycle writes the same block again whenever the outcome set changes.
2. **Counterfactual.** Each blocked trade is replayed the way the backtest the strategy was admitted on would
   have executed it: decision on the blocked session's bar, entry at the next session's open, then the
   strategy row's own tested exit plan (`strategy_execution_policy.exit_policy_for`: 3% stop / 6% target /
   20 sessions for the technical strategies; 10% stop / 10 sessions / indicator exit for RSI(2) and IBS) over
   the stored trusted daily bars, with `triple_barrier.label_events` costs on both sides (volatility-scaled
   slippage plus fees, stop before target, gaps at the open). Where 1-minute bars exist for the entry session
   the opening print replaces the daily open. A trade still inside its horizon is `open`, not scored; a symbol
   without trusted bars or a strategy without a tested exit plan is `unscorable`.
3. **Verdict per gate and per strategy.** Blocked, scored and open counts, mean net return, win rate, the net
   profit the gate forfeited (blocked winners) against the losses it avoided (blocked losers), in bps and in
   dollars at the cost model's nominal order size. With at least 30 scored trades (`gate_cost_min_scored`) a
   positive mean is `costly` and a negative one `earning its keep`; fewer is `too early`.
4. **Where it lives.** One `gate_cost` audit row per completed session (no migration), written by
   `gate_cost_job` on the learning queue at 17:20 ET on weekdays, after the last trade-attribution run (16:35),
   loss feedback (16:45) and the experiment verdict (17:10). `GET /stock-paper/gate-costs` (viewer) returns the
   latest row; the learning scoreboard gained a `gate_costs` section.
5. **The engine hook.** A gate flagged `costly` for a strategy becomes a lesson for the experiment engine
   (`costly_gate_lessons`), with cause `costly_market_gate` / `costly_vix_gate` /
   `costly_earnings_blackout`. These causes were added to the matching templates' triggers, so the engine plans
   the same replay it runs for a loss lesson, with "off" among the candidates as before, and adopts a change
   only under its unchanged pre-registered rule (`experiment_engine.RULE`). A gate the audit calls costly on 60
   sessions of paper evidence is therefore only removed if the full-history replay agrees.
6. **VIX and regime gates are now counted.** Those two gates live inside the strategy wrapper and turned a BUY
   into a HOLD before the cycle saw it, so they never appeared among the withheld BUYs. The cycle now records
   such a gated HOLD as a skipped BUY with the gate's reason (`gated_action` on the signal result); the order
   path is unchanged.

Tests: `tests/test_gate_cost.py` (18) cover scoring from synthetic bars under both exit plans, the intraday
opening print, the open/scored/unscorable split, dedup, gate naming, the session window, the costly / earning /
too-early labels, the stored report and both views, the engine hook end to end (a costly market gate turns the
gate off under the fixed rule) and the job, queue, route-role and setting wiring.

## What it cannot measure yet

* **Intraday fills.** The paper cycle would have bought at the signal's intraday reference price during the
  blocked session, not at the next open. The next-open model matches the backtest the strategies were admitted
  on and the engine's own replay, but it is not the fill the bot would have got. Scoring the same-session fill
  needs the minute bars from the moment of the block to the close, which the feed keeps only for the symbols it
  tracks; the opening print is used where it exists and nothing more.
* **Blocks the cycle never recorded.** Outcome rows are written only when the outcome set changes, so a block
  that persists unchanged across sessions is recorded on its first session only, and a signal withheld by the
  daily-bar entry window before any gate ran is not a gate block. The counts are a floor.
* **Position-level effects.** A blocked trade is scored on its own. The slot it would have taken, the
  correlation with the positions held that day and the sizing the gauges would have applied are not modelled,
  so "forfeited profit" is per-trade, not portfolio profit.
* **Gates without an engine template.** `stop_cooldown`, `sector_cap`, `one_position_per_symbol`,
  `announcement_blackout`, `regime_gate` and the rest get a measured cost and a verdict but no automatic
  experiment; a costly verdict on one of them is a lesson for a person until the roadmap's later items give them
  templates.
* **Evidence size.** Thirty scored trades per gate and strategy over 60 sessions will take weeks for the
  rarer gates; until then every pair reads `too early`, by design.
