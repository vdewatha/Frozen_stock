# Slow book with a passive core: 2026-11-07

**What this is:** step 2 of the build plan in [Retail bot edge feasibility](Retail%20bot%20edge%20feasibility.md) ("build the slow book with a passive core"). It is a live paper change. Once it is published, the bot puts the capital its other strategies leave unused into a passive S&P 500 / T-bill arm, in four monthly tranches. The seven broad technical strategies and RSI(2)'s stock leg stop trading and keep recording signals. Paper only: nothing here can reach a live account.

Code: `app/services/target_weight_book.py` (the engine; its docstring is the reference), with small, scoped changes to `stock_paper_ledger.py`, `risk.py`, `stock_paper_autotrader.py`, `stock_exit_rearm.py`, `stock_monitoring.py`, `trading_universe.py`, `seed.py`, the jobs, the beat schedule and one viewer endpoint. No database migration: state lives in audit rows and strategy-row parameters.

## What was built

* **A target-weight sleeve engine outside the slot and bracket system.** A sleeve is a strategy row whose parameters carry `sleeve_cadence: "monthly"` and a `target_weights` map. The step 1 verdict code reads `sleeve_cadence` to keep sleeves out of the 20-trade revert. A sleeve with capital has status `target_weight_active`, which the slot cycle, the experiment engine and the allocation code never select. The engine nets every sleeve's target per symbol, compares it with the broker holding and sends one marketable DAY limit order per symbol (the decision price plus or minus 20 bps). Sells go first, and buys are bounded by free cash. Orders use the existing reservation and dispatch path, with every launch, feed, reconciliation and kill-switch gate unchanged. Each order carries source `target_weight_book`, the sleeve as its strategy and a numbered idempotency key `tw:<month>:t<k>:<symbol>:<side>:<n>` (the PR #17 pattern). Quantities are fractional where Alpaca lists the asset as fractionable and whole shares otherwise; an asset Alpaca does not list as tradable blocks the rebalance. After the close, each fill is benchmarked against the delayed SIP tape: the minute bar containing the fill and the session's SIP close (`fill_benchmark` rows).
* **Four weekly tranches.** Tranche *k* rebalances on the *k*-th Tuesday of each month (setting `target_weight_rebalance_weekday`), or on the next session that week after a holiday, so a quarter of the book turns each week. A tranche's capital is a quarter of the free capital, bounded by what the other three tranches leave free. The latest `tranche_rebalance` audit row for each tranche holds its shares per sleeve per symbol. The book target is the sum over tranches, and the engine splits its netted positions back to sleeves by target shares for attribution. The job runs at 10:30, 11:30 and 13:00 ET on weekdays, clear of the opening and closing auctions. The first run plans the tranche. The later runs that day send whatever is still missing, for example buys that waited for sale proceeds to reconcile into cash.
* **Passive arm sleeve.** The `passive_arm` row (created once by the seed, status `target_weight_active`) holds every unassigned dollar in **VOO** (equity) and **SGOV** (0-3 month T-bills). The weight comes from `passive_arm_equity_weight` (default 0.20 of the arm) and the rest goes to SGOV. VOO and SGOV are listed, liquid, fractionable ETFs on Alpaca; the engine still checks `GET /v2/assets/<symbol>` before trading. The settings refuse SPY, QQQ, IWM and DIA for the arm. The book's symbols are removed from the slot cycle (and from exit re-arm), so IBS and RSI(2) are never blocked by the one-position-per-symbol rule, and every broker position belongs to exactly one side.
* **Modeled distributions.** Alpaca paper does not simulate dividends, so the daily job books them from yfinance dividend history: for each ex-date, the shares held at the prior close times the amount per share, once per symbol and ex-date, split to sleeves (`distribution_accrual` rows). These are modeled entries, not cash.
* **Daily sleeve P&L for the step 1 harm guardrail.** One `sleeve_daily_pnl` row per session (event `target_weight_book`). `payload.sleeves[<strategy id>]` holds `pnl`, `distributions`, `value` and `return`. `payload.passive_arm` holds the passive-arm return series. The row also records the book's total and a same-weights replay: the intended shares, traded at the decision price and valued at the daily close, with modeled distributions. The importer withholds a daily bar until the next UTC day, so a session's row is written by the next morning's run (08:45 ET); the job also runs at 17:45 ET.
* **Turnover budget.** The engine tracks the trailing 12-month one-sided turnover of its own fills (half of buys plus sells over average equity). Above the soft budget (8 times equity) the rebalance band doubles. At the hard cap (10) only risk-reducing sells go out. The band is 2% of a tranche's capital: a smaller change keeps its old shares.
* **Viewer endpoint.** `GET /stock-paper/target-weight-book` (viewer role) shows the engine state, the exemption, settings, sleeves, free capital, per-tranche state, book targets, holdings with their sleeve split, this month's tranche schedule, turnover and the band in force, cycle verdicts and the pass clock, plus recent checks, daily P&L, distributions, fill benchmarks and orders. Two admin posts sit beside it: `/target-weight-book/resume` and `/target-weight-book/exemption`.

## How capital flows

Free capital for the book is equity, less what the slot strategies hold or are buying, less `target_weight_slot_reserve` (10% of equity, kept for IBS and RSI(2) entries and exploration lots), less a 0.5% cash buffer, and never more than the 100% gross cap allows. On a tranche day, that tranche takes a quarter of the free capital: 20% into VOO and 80% into SGOV. With the seven technical strategies in shadow, the slot side holds little, so after the first four weeks the book should sit near 88-90% of equity: about 18% VOO and 71% SGOV. When IBS or RSI(2) open positions, the next tranche simply sizes smaller. The book never sells to make room for them, because the reserve already does that.

In the first month the book builds up a quarter at a time, starting with the tranche whose week a publish lands in, so capital is fully deployed after about four weeks.

## The risk-rule change

Target-weight BUYs are exempt from the **1%-of-equity-per-BUY** and **10%-per-symbol** caps. They keep the **100% gross-exposure cap** (counting pending buys), the kill switch and the daily drawdown limit (`risk.approve_target_weight_order`). The exemption is the `strategy_governance` action `target_weight_risk_exemption`. It is granted once by the seed, and it is visible and reversible: `POST /stock-paper/target-weight-book/exemption {"active": false}` writes `target_weight_risk_exemption_revoked`, after which target-weight buys face both caps again, and the seed never re-grants it. The exemption is scoped to orders whose source is `target_weight_book`, that name an active sleeve and that trade one of the book's own symbols. A scheduled or exploration order is untouched (tests cover this), and the ledger refuses a target-weight order for any other symbol or strategy. The book's positions also sit outside the slot open-position counts.

Every holding must sit within **1 point of equity** of its target after its tranche rebalances (`tranche_check` rows). Monitoring follows the same split. The concentration and turnover checks watch the slot strategies. The gross-exposure check still breaches at the whole book's 100% cap, while its warning band reads the slot side only, so a fully deployed passive arm does not hold every resume on a permanent warning.

## What moved to shadow

A new status, `shadow`, keeps a strategy in the signal cycle, so every signal is recorded, but it places no entries, scheduled or exploration, and takes no learning budget. A one-time seed step (`slow_book_shadow` governance rows) moves MA crossover, MACD, ensemble, RSI(14), Bollinger, channel breakout and trend pullback to `shadow`. It acts only on rows that are still trading or exploring and have no earlier event. A paused or retired row stays as it is (MACD and MA crossover were already retired by the 2026-10-28 scorecard), and a later operator change is never overridden. RSI(2) gets a `shadow_symbols` list of its 29 single stocks (`rsi2_stock_leg_shadow`). It keeps trading SPY, QQQ, IWM and DIA and still records its stock signals. IBS is unchanged. Open positions of shadowed strategies exit by their normal rules (broker legs, time stops, the protective stop, and their own SELL on an attributed lot) and are not force-sold.

## The pass rule

The rule is computed monthly. The first three **complete** monthly cycles (all four tranches rebalanced) must each show:

1. every post-tranche check within 1 point of target;
2. the month's paper return within **15 bps** of the same-weights replay, both after modeled distributions;
3. turnover inside the budget: the month's one-sided turnover excluding first funding, annualized, at most the soft budget, and the trailing 12 months under the hard cap.

A `cycle_verdict` row records each month. On a failure the engine pauses (`engine_pause`, with the reasons and a notification) and holds: it sends no further rebalance orders, keeps its current holdings and leaves new money in cash. `POST /stock-paper/target-weight-book/resume` (admin) restores the passive arm's status if a pause-all moved it, and restarts the three-cycle clock.

## Settings and defaults

| Setting | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `TARGET_WEIGHT_BOOK_ENABLED` | `true` | the engine runs (the chosen live paper change) |
| `PASSIVE_ARM_EQUITY_WEIGHT` | `0.20` | the arm's equity share, a risk budget the owner sets |
| `PASSIVE_ARM_EQUITY_SYMBOL` | `VOO` | S&P 500 fund; never SPY, QQQ, IWM or DIA |
| `PASSIVE_ARM_TBILL_SYMBOL` | `SGOV` | T-bill ETF |
| `TARGET_WEIGHT_REBALANCE_BAND` | `0.02` | a change under 2% of a tranche's capital keeps the old shares |
| `TARGET_WEIGHT_BAND_WIDEN_FACTOR` | `2.0` | band multiplier above the soft budget |
| `TARGET_WEIGHT_TURNOVER_SOFT` / `_HARD` | `8.0` / `10.0` | one-sided turnover per year, times equity |
| `TARGET_WEIGHT_SLOT_RESERVE` | `0.10` | equity kept free for the slot strategies |
| `TARGET_WEIGHT_CASH_BUFFER` | `0.005` | cash kept for the limit collar and price moves |
| `TARGET_WEIGHT_REBALANCE_WEEKDAY` | `1` (Tuesday) | tranche *k* = the *k*-th such weekday |
| `TARGET_WEIGHT_LIMIT_COLLAR_BPS` | `20` | marketable limit distance from the decision price |
| `TARGET_WEIGHT_WEIGHT_TOLERANCE` | `0.01` | post-tranche holding gap, fraction of equity |
| `TARGET_WEIGHT_TRACKING_TOLERANCE_BPS` | `15` | monthly paper vs replay gap |
| `TARGET_WEIGHT_PASS_CYCLES` | `3` | complete cycles the pass rule needs |

## Interpretations and limits

* **"All unassigned capital" leaves a 10% reserve.** Without it, IBS and RSI(2) entries would be refused for cash or by the gross cap between tranches.
* **"Within 1 point of target" is checked against what the engine meant to hold** (the summed tranche shares at the close), not against the ideal weights. Between tranches, the other three tranches drift with the market by design, and a market move must not read as an implementation fault.
* **Turnover is the book's own.** The engine controls only its own trades; the account-wide figure stays on the existing monitoring check.
* **A pause holds; it does not liquidate.** Selling everything on an implementation fault would add turnover and risk. The owner can still close positions manually.
* **The replay is an implementation check, not a benchmark.** It uses the same intended shares at the decision price, so the gap measures fills, rejections and cash drag, not market timing.
