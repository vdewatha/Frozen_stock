# VIX Market-Fear Gate, 2026-11-06

**Question:** the loss review's most common missed cause is a market-wide move. Does the bot do better if a
strategy only buys when the market's fear gauge (the VIX) is in the right range?

**Goal used (changed 2026-11-06):** total profit after costs, not win rate.

**Decision rule:** turn a gate on only if, in all three windows:
- net expectancy per trade is not worse;
- total profit (trades × expectancy) is not lower;
- portfolio max drawdown is not worse.

**Answer:** the simple idea "don't buy when fear is high" is wrong for the bot's best strategy. Each strategy
type wants the opposite of the other:

| Strategy | Gate | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| IBS Mean Reversion | `above` 20 (buy only when VIX ≥ 20) | **Turn on** | Expectancy +15.4 → +33.6, +21.3 → +60.4, +23.9 → +50.9 bps. Total profit up in all three windows. Drawdown 2.0 → 1.8%, 1.2 → 1.1%, 1.2 → 1.1%. Its CI excludes zero in every window |
| Channel Breakout | `below` 20 (buy only when VIX < 20) | **Turn on** | Expectancy −2.6 → +6.9, +17.3 → +22.4, +20.7 → +31.5 bps. Total profit up in all three windows. Drawdown 5.7 → 2.2%, 2.3 → 2.2%, 1.7 → 1.2% |
| RSI(2) Pullback | `above` 20 would help per trade | Keep off | Expectancy doubles (+24.9 → +50.6 on full history), but it keeps only 41% of the trades, so total profit falls on full history and since 2020 |
| RSI Mean Reversion (ADX range-gated) | none | Keep off | `below` 20 helps on full history but is worse since 2015 and since 2020 |
| Bollinger Mean Reversion (ADX range-gated) | none | Keep off | Every gate is worse in at least one window |
| Trend Pullback | `not_rising` passes | Keep off | It passes, but the strategy has about zero edge either way (+4.4, +13.9, +0.1 bps) |

**Why it flips:** IBS and RSI(2) buy a 1–2 day dip. When fear is high, dips are deeper and snap back harder,
which is the same reason the ADX trend gate hurt them. Breakouts need calm markets: in a fearful market a
breakout is more often a reversal that fails.

**What this means for losses:** a "market moved against us" loss on an IBS trade bought in a calm market is
what the gate removes. Breakouts bought in a fearful market are removed too. RSI(2) dips bought in calm markets
are still taken on purpose: they win less often but add total profit.

**Caveats:**
- The 45 symbols are ones that exist today, so survivorship bias makes every number look better than reality.
- The three windows overlap.
- 30 gated variants were tested without a multiple-testing correction, with round thresholds (20/25/30) that
  were not tuned.
- Every gated CI overlaps its ungated CI. Treat "turn on" as "no evidence against, and the mechanism makes
  sense", not as a proven edge.
- Compare paper results with these numbers in the weekly report.

## The gate

`app/services/strategies/vix_gate.py` reads the CBOE VIX close dated on the last completed daily bar.
- It falls back to the previous close on a VIX holiday, and reads up to 5 days back at most.
- The close is known by 16:15 New York time, before the next open the backtester fills at, so there is no
  lookahead.
- A strategy row opts in with `vix_gate`:
  - `below`: BUY only when VIX < `vix_level`, default 25;
  - `above`: BUY only when VIX ≥ `vix_level`, default 20;
  - `not_rising`: BUY unless VIX rose more than `vix_rise_pct` (10%) over `vix_rise_days` (5) sessions.
- It stacks with the ADX `regime_gate` (both are applied in `registry.get_strategy`). It only turns a BUY into
  a HOLD; sells and exits are never touched.
- Every gated BUY records the reading in `features.vix_gate`.
- **Fails open:** with no VIX close within 5 days, the BUY passes, flagged `vix_unavailable`. This way a
  yfinance outage can't silently stop a strategy.
- **History source:** a yfinance `^VIX` download, cached for 6 hours per process. A failed download is retried
  after 15 minutes. `VIX_HISTORY_CSV` overrides it for backtests.

A one-time seed step (`enable_vix_gates`, audit action `vix_gate_update`) sets the two gates above on their
strategy rows. Like the ADX step, it skips a row that already has `vix_gate` or an earlier update event, so a
later operator change is kept.

## Data and method

- **Data:** yfinance daily OHLCV, split/dividend adjusted, from the 2026-11-02 scratch cache used by
  `regime-gate-20261106.md`, for the 45 symbols in `settings.paper_execution_symbols`, through 2026-11-02.
  VIX: yfinance `^VIX` daily closes from 1990-01-02 (9,283 days).
- **Engine:** `strategy_scorecard.run_scorecard`, the same backtester and costs as the scorecard and the ADX
  report. Signals are computed on completed bars and filled at the next open, with volatility-scaled costs on
  each side.
- **Strategy settings:**
  - RSI(2) and IBS use their seeded settings, with the 10% catastrophe stop and 10-session time stop.
  - RSI(14) and Bollinger reversion carry their live ADX `range` gate.
  - Channel Breakout and Trend Pullback use defaults.
- **Variants:** 36 (6 strategies × none, below 20/25/30, above 20, not_rising) × 45 symbols = 1,620 backtests,
  52 minutes.
- **Windows:** all history per symbol, 2015+, 2020+. Trades are pooled across symbols.
  - Expectancy is the mean net return per trade in bps, with a 95% cluster-bootstrap CI by entry month.
  - Port DD is the max drawdown of the equal-weight daily portfolio.
  - Sym DD is the mean per-symbol max drawdown.
- **Script:** scratch only (`vix_gate_bt.py`, not committed). It is the ADX report's script with
  `{"vix_gate": ...}` variants and `VIX_HISTORY_CSV` set.
